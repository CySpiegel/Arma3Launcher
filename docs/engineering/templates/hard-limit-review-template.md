A proposal is not approval. Keep acceptance/merge blocked until the user decides.

- Responsibilities and adopted pattern/architecture binding:

- Feasible extraction/decomposition/redesign options and tradeoffs:

- Human decision: pending | approved | declined (agents cannot approve):

- Approved bounds (only after actual decision), evidence and invalidation conditions:
